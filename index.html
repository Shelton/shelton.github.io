Before this

"Keep it simple," said many of my English teachers and design professors. So: I spent years in industrial design and in user experience research and design, led empathy trainings, and helped build communities.

The job was always the same. Listen closely, figure out what people actually need, and help a group see options it couldn't see alone. Mediation is that job with higher stakes.
