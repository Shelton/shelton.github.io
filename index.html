Hello!

"Keep it simple," said many of my English teachers and design professors, so here goes.

I help people get unstuck. For years that meant designing products and services. These days it mostly means mediation: helping families, teams, partners and neighbors slow down, really listen, and sketch out what comes next together. I'm working toward certification in domestic mediation.

I'm a son, friend, husband, father, founder, researcher, competitive athlete, and always ready for a set of hurdles (110HH, day two, it won't be pretty).

What I'm up to

SD Mediates

My mediation practice, in person or by video. Less me vs. you. More us vs. the problem.

DivorceSucks.wtf

Field research. I talk with people whose divorce is final about what they wish they'd known, then turn the patterns into tools for whoever's next.

Before this

I spent years in industrial design and in user experience research and design, led empathy trainings, and helped build communities.
